OUR VISION

“To be the Global Benchmark for a Capital City”

Our vision for Delhi as a Smart City is to become a model for other global capitals — focused on people-first development, sustainable urban systems, and responsive governance.

  • 💡 A connected, digitally empowered city with smart mobility and e-governance.
  • 🌿 A green and clean environment with strong air, water and waste management.
  • 🏙️ Inclusive public spaces and universal accessibility for all communities.
  • 📊 Data-driven decisions and transparent systems using real-time monitoring.
  • 🧠 Innovation in service delivery to uplift every citizen’s quality of life.

This vision is aligned with NDMC’s Smart City Mission, emphasizing citizen participation, infrastructure upgrades, and long-term resilience for India’s proud capital.
